## What is AcelleMail?

AcelleMail is self-hosted email marketing software built with **PHP and Laravel**.

Install it on your own Linux server and take complete control of your email marketing infrastructure, customer data, campaigns, and sending configuration.

### Why AcelleMail?

- 🖥️ **Self-hosted** — run AcelleMail on your own server
- 🔓 **Full source code** — customize and extend the platform
- 💰 **One-time purchase** — no recurring subscription for the software
- 📧 **Unlimited email sending** — send through your preferred SMTP or delivery provider
- 👥 **Unlimited subscribers** — manage your contacts without per-subscriber pricing
- ⚡ **Marketing automation** — welcome series, drip campaigns, triggers, and re-engagement
- 🎨 **Drag-and-drop email builder** — create responsive campaigns without coding
- 📊 **Analytics & reporting** — track opens, clicks, bounces, and campaign performance
- 🔌 **Powerful integrations** — Amazon SES, SendGrid, SparkPost, Mailgun, Postmark, Elastic Email, and standard SMTP
- 🏢 **SaaS & multi-tenancy** — build and operate your own email marketing SaaS
- 🏷️ **White-label ready** — customize the platform for your own brand
- 🤖 **AI capabilities** — extend AcelleMail with AI-powered content and deliverability tools

## Email Marketing Features

### 🎨 Email Builder

Create professional email campaigns with a visual drag-and-drop editor and 100+ responsive templates.

### ⚙️ Marketing Automation

Build automated workflows including:

- Welcome sequences
- Drip campaigns
- Triggered emails
- Re-engagement campaigns
- Date-based campaigns
- Behavioral automation

### 👥 Contact & List Management
